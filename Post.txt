♾️ What comes before Infinity, and what comes after it?

Before infinity: Infinity. ♾️

Infinity itself: No finite endpoint.

After infinity: There is no immediate next number in the ordinary sense of the word infinity.


But mathematics has a fascinating twist! 🌌

There are different sizes of infinity. For example, the infinity of real numbers is larger than the infinity of natural numbers.

∞ → ∞ → ∞

Infinity isn't the end of the number line. It's a concept that challenges our understanding of limits.

The ultimate question: If infinity has no end, how can anything be beyond it? 🚀
